WORDWHISTLE

Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

WordWhistle is a parent-controlled spelling practice app. It works without an account and keeps spelling lists and practice results on the device.

Information we collect

WordWhistle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, child profiles, social features, or third-party sign-in.

Parents can enter spelling words and list titles. The app stores those entries, practice attempts, and settings only on the device using local application storage. This information is not sent to WordWhistle or to a cloud service.

Audio and illustrations

Spoken-word playback uses an eligible voice installed on the device. WordWhistle does not send spelling words to a hosted speech service. Illustrations included in this version are bundled with the app and do not require uploading words or images.

Device backups

The device operating system or a service configured by the device owner may include application data in a device backup. Those backups are controlled by the device owner and the applicable platform provider, not by WordWhistle.

Deleting information

A parent can delete individual spelling lists or erase all WordWhistle lists and results from Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local application data, subject to any device backups controlled by the device owner.

Children’s privacy

WordWhistle does not ask a child for a name, email address, age, location, photo, recording, or other personal information. The app is intended to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian.

Changes

If a future version adds an online service or changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
